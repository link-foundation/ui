## Context

The comment on issue 101 asks for full client and server support of the
associative architecture "in doublets and triplets". link-cli plans triplets
stored in doublets ({{L04}}) and served over the Links Notation text and
binary protocol ({{L05}}). The client side here needs the same model.

## Goal

A triplets view over any ui-links storage, and a synced triplets client
over the triplet protocol.

## In scope

1. `withTriplets(links)` in JavaScript with the encoding chosen in {{L04}}
   (the tests of both repositories share fixtures).
2. Substitution queries with three-part links in `runQuery`.
3. The synced client with the triplet operations of {{L05}}.

## Acceptance criteria

- The triplet conversation of link-cli's `docs/protocol/` replays against
  the JavaScript server.
- A synced triplets client creates, reads and deletes triplets through
  `clink --serve --triplets`.
