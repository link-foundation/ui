## Context

Deep-foundation's links are triplets: `(id, type, from, to)`, where the type
is a link too. link-cli stores doublets `(id: source target)`. To serve the
triplets architecture without a second storage engine, triplets can be
stored in doublets, as the comment on link-foundation/ui#101 asks:
"a compatibility layer of triplets stored in doublets, providing similar
associative API".

## Goal

A decorator `TripletsOverDoublets` (C# and Rust) with an associative API of
triplets (`Create(type, from, to)`, `Update`, `Delete`, `Each`, `Count`,
substitution with a type part) over any doublets `ILinks`.

## In scope

1. Choose and document the encoding. Two candidates:
   - **type as source** (the one of link-foundation/ui's `typeSystem`): the
     triplet `(i: T f t)` is the doublet `(i: T p)` with `p` the pair
     `(p: f t)`, so the type of the triplet is its source and the
     type-system queries of {{L01}} work on triplets as they are;
   - **pair of pairs**: `(i: (T) (f t))` with marker links;
     compare the number of doublets per triplet, the cost of `Each` by type,
     from or to, and deletion (shared pairs must be counted or kept).
2. Keep the triplet address stable when its parts change (update the outer
   doublet, collect the inner pair when it is no longer used).
3. Substitution queries with three parts: `((i: T f t)) ((i: T f t2))`.
4. LiNo import and export of triplets.

## Out of scope

The text and binary protocol for triplets ({{L05}}), GraphQL ({{L06}}).

## Acceptance criteria

- Creating, reading, updating and deleting a triplet leaves no orphan
  doublets.
- `Each` by type, by from and by to returns the same triplets as a triplet
  store would, on a test set of at least 1000 triplets.
- The encoding and its costs are documented in `docs/`.
