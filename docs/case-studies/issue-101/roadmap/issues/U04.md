## Context

A session of `createLinksServer` pushes to its subscriptions the changed
links that match and that its subject may read after the change
(`deliver` and `fits` in `packages/ui-links/src/server.js`). When a link
changes type, the subscriber sees it appear or disappear, as the test
"serves each subject what its permissions allow" checks. But when a **rule**
or the **type hierarchy** changes (a new `(r: AllowRead g)`, a removed
`Extends`), only the rule link itself changes, so a subscriber does not
receive the links that just became readable and keeps the links that just
became hidden.

## Goal

After a change of rules or of the hierarchy, each subscriber receives the
links that its subscriptions match and that became readable (as creations)
or unreadable (as deletions).

## In scope

1. Detect writes that touch rule links, permission markers or `Extends`
   links (the policy knows its markers).
2. For each session with subscriptions, compute the difference of the
   readable matching links before and after; push it as one `(notify: n)`.
3. Keep it cheap: only sessions whose subject a changed rule mentions, or
   all sessions when a rule for `Anything` changes; a cache of the readable
   types per subject.

## Acceptance criteria

- A regression test: alice subscribes to `(* * *)`, the boss adds "User
  reads Secret"; alice receives the secret links; the boss removes it; alice
  receives their deletion.
- The existing server and client tests pass unchanged.
