## Context

The UI of deep-foundation (deepcase) and its client (`DeepClient` of
`@deep-foundation/deeplinks`) talk to Hasura (`hasura/graphql-engine`
v2.43 in deeplinks' docker-compose) over GraphQL at `/gql`: queries,
mutations and subscriptions on the tables `links`, `strings`, `numbers`,
`objects`, `can`, `selectors`, `tree` (`mp`) and `handlers`, with Hasura's
`where` (`bool_exp`), `order_by`, `limit`, `offset` and nested inserts.
There is no written "Deep Standard" and no published schema: the standard
is, in practice, the schema Hasura generates from deeplinks' migrations
(12 tracked tables, 37 relationships, 2 computed fields, a `reserve`
action, `jwt`/`guest` remote schemas, row permissions through `can`).

The comment on link-foundation/ui#101 asks for a "Hasura reimplementation of
Deep Standard, so the UI of deep-foundation would be able to connect to
link-cli as a server", with Deep packages optional. It extends
link-foundation/link-cli#34 (a GraphQL LINO API).

## Goal

A GraphQL endpoint in link-cli that implements the subset of Hasura's schema
that `DeepClient` uses, over triplets stored in doublets.

## In scope

1. Record Hasura's introspection of a fresh deeplinks database as the
   target schema, and the GraphQL operations `DeepClient` sends (`select`,
   `insert`, `update`, `delete`, `serial`, subscriptions, the `jwt` and
   `guest` auth actions) as fixtures.
2. Map the `links` table to triplets ({{L04}}), `strings`/`numbers`/`objects`
   to values linked to their owner, `can` to the permission layer
   ({{L02}}), subscriptions to {{L03}}.
3. Hasura's `where` (`_eq`, `_in`, `_and`, `_or`, `_not`, relation filters
   `from`, `to`, `type`, `in`, `out`, `typed`) translated to substitution
   queries.
4. Subscriptions over WebSocket in the protocol deepcase uses today
   (`apollo-link-ws`, i.e. the legacy `subscriptions-transport-ws`), and
   `graphql-ws` for newer clients; `Authorization` and the
   `x-hasura-user-id` claim of the JWT carry the subject.
5. Optional loading of Deep packages (`@deep-foundation/core` types) as
   links.

## Out of scope

Deep handlers (the code execution of deep-foundation's packages).

## Acceptance criteria

- `DeepClient` from `@deep-foundation/deeplinks` connects to
  `clink --serve --graphql`, logs in as guest, selects, inserts and deletes
  links, and receives a subscription update.
- deepcase opens and shows the links of a fresh database.
